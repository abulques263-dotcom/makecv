How to use this calculator

  1. Enter your CGPA.
  2. Select the conversion formula or enter a custom maximum and multiplier.
  3. Calculate and compare the result with your institution's official guidance.
Formula examples: CGPA × 9.5, CGPA ÷ 10 × 100, or CGPA ÷ 4 × 100. These are not interchangeable official rules. Use the formula specified by your institution.

Frequently asked questions

Is one CGPA formula valid for every university?

No. Conversion methods differ. This tool shows an estimate for the formula you select; check your transcript guidance or university policy.

Can I use a 4-point CGPA?

Yes. Select the 4-point linear example and enter a CGPA from 0 to 4, if that formula suits your institution.

Does Calcora store my CGPA?

The calculator runs in your browser and does not need an account. Avoid entering private information into any public website.
