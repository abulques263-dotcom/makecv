Estimate simple interest and the total amount for a fixed annual interest rate.

How to use this calculator

  1. Enter the starting principal amount.
  2. Enter the annual interest rate as a percentage.
  3. Enter the time period in years and calculate.
Simple interest (SI) = P × R × T ÷ 100. Total amount = P + SI, where P is principal, R is annual rate in percent and T is time in years.

Frequently asked questions

Does this calculate compound interest?

No. It calculates simple interest, which is based on the original principal.

Can the rate be zero?

Yes. The interest will be zero and the total will equal the principal.

What if my time is in months?

Convert months to years by dividing the number of months by 12.
