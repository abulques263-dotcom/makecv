How to use this calculator

  1. Enter the original price.
  2. Enter a discount from 0% to 100%.
  3. Calculate to see the final price and savings.
Savings = original price × discount percentage ÷ 100. Final price = original price − savings. Taxes and extra fees are not included.

Frequently asked questions

Can I enter a 100% discount?

Yes. The final price will be zero before taxes or fees.

Does it include tax?

No. Taxes, shipping and extra fees are not included.

Can I use decimals?

Yes. Decimal prices and discount percentages are supported.
