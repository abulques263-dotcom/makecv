About Calcora

Smart calculations. Simple solutions.

What is Calcora?

Calcora is a small collection of free, easy-to-use calculators designed to make everyday calculations more straightforward. Current tools cover marks and percentages, CGPA conversion estimates, required marks, averages, grades, age, dates, discounts, simple interest and exam countdowns.

Our approach

  • Simple: clear inputs and results without a sign-up.
  • Browser-based: calculations are performed in your browser for the tools on this site.
  • Transparent: formulas and important assumptions are explained where relevant.
  • Mobile-friendly: pages are designed to adapt to smaller screens.

Important limitations

Results are for general information and planning. Schools and universities may use their own CGPA, grade and rounding rules, so always confirm official requirements with the relevant institution. Financial calculators are estimates and do not replace professional advice.
