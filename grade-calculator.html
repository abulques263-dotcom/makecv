How to use this calculator

  1. Enter your score and the maximum possible score.
  2. Choose an example grade scale.
  3. Calculate the percentage and estimated letter grade.
Percentage = score ÷ maximum score × 100. Example grade boundaries are illustrative only and may not match your school, university, course, or country.

Frequently asked questions

Are these official grade boundaries?

No. Grading systems vary by school, course and country. Treat the output as an example and compare it with your course's published scale.

Can the maximum score be different from 100?

Yes. Enter the actual maximum score and the calculator converts it to a percentage first.

Does the calculator round before assigning a grade?

It uses the calculated percentage and the selected example thresholds; displayed rounding is only for presentation.
