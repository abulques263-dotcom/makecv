How to use this calculator

  1. Choose the date of your exam.
  2. Calculate to see days remaining, or whether the date has passed.
Days remaining = exam calendar date minus today's calendar date. This countdown uses your device's local date and does not include the current day as a full remaining day.

Frequently asked questions

Does the countdown update automatically?

Recalculate to refresh it against today's date.

Can I choose a past exam date?

Yes. The tool will tell you how many days ago it was.

Does it send reminders?

No. This is a simple in-browser countdown calculator, not a reminder service.
