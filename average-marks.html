Average Marks Calculator

Calculate the arithmetic average of a list of marks. Enter scores separated by commas, spaces or new lines.

Your result

How to use this calculator

  1. Enter at least one non-negative mark.
  2. Separate values with commas or new lines.
  3. Calculate to see the average and total.
Average = sum of all entered values ÷ number of values. This is a simple arithmetic mean; it does not account for different subject weights or maximum marks.
