Find how many marks you need in the remaining assessment to reach your target final percentage.

Your result

How to use this calculator

  1. Enter marks scored so far and the maximum marks completed.
  2. Enter the maximum marks still available.
  3. Set your target final percentage and calculate.
Required marks = (target percentage × total final maximum marks) − marks already scored. This assumes all listed assessments count according to their maximum marks.

Frequently asked questions

What if the target is impossible?

The result explains when required marks exceed the remaining maximum available.

Does this work with weighted exams?

Only if the entered maximum marks represent the actual weighting. For weighted grading, use official weighted totals.

Can I enter a 100% target?

Yes. The calculator checks whether the remaining marks can achieve it.
