About BMI

Body Mass Index (BMI) is calculated from weight and height. It is a broad screening measure and cannot distinguish muscle from fat or assess health on its own. Gender does not change the standard adult BMI cutoffs; age is important when interpreting BMI for children and teens.

How BMI is calculated

Metric: BMI = weight in kilograms ÷ (height in metres × height in metres).

US units: BMI = 703 × weight in pounds ÷ (height in inches × height in inches).

What are BMI Prime and Ponderal Index?

BMI Prime compares BMI with the upper end of the usual adult reference range (25). Ponderal Index uses weight and height cubed. These are additional mathematical measures, not diagnoses.

Frequently asked questions

Does gender change the adult BMI category?

The standard adult BMI cutoffs are the same for men and women. Gender is included here as a reference field, not as a separate adult cutoff.

Can I use this calculator for a teenager?

You can calculate the BMI number, but children and teenagers need age- and sex-specific growth charts for interpretation. This page will not label their result using adult categories.

Is BMI a diagnosis?

No. BMI is a screening measure and should not be used on its own to judge health.
