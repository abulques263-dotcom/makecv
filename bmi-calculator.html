How BMI is calculated

BMI = weight in kilograms ÷ (height in metres × height in metres).

BMI is a screening measure, not a diagnosis. It does not distinguish muscle from fat or describe health on its own. For children and teenagers, interpretation uses age- and sex-specific growth charts rather than adult BMI cut-offs. If you are under 18, ask a qualified healthcare professional to interpret the result; don't use this number alone to judge your health.

Frequently asked questions

What does BMI mean?

BMI is weight in kilograms divided by height in metres squared. It is a screening measure and not a complete assessment of health.

Are adult BMI categories suitable for teens?

No. Children and teenagers need age- and sex-specific interpretation by an appropriate health professional.

Does BMI diagnose a health condition?

No. A healthcare professional considers other information to understand health.
