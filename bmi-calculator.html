Adult BMI categories

For adults aged 18 and over, BMI is grouped into these general ranges.

Below usual rangeBelow 18.5
Usual adult range18.5–24.9
Overweight range25.0–29.9
Obesity range30 and above
Important to know

BMI is a screening measure, not a diagnosis. It does not measure health on its own.

Frequently asked questions

How is BMI calculated?

BMI is calculated by dividing weight in kilograms by height in metres squared.

Are adult BMI categories suitable for teenagers?

No. Children and teenagers need age- and sex-specific growth charts. This calculator will not assign an adult category to anyone under 18.

Is BMI a diagnosis?

No. BMI is a screening measure only and should not be used on its own to judge health.
