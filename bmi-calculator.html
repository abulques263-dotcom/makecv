BMI reference categories

The table below shows commonly used adult BMI categories. BMI is a screening measure, not a diagnosis, and does not directly measure body fat or overall health.

CategoryBMI (kg/m²)
Severe Thinness< 16
Moderate Thinness16 – 16.9
Mild Thinness17 – 18.4
Normal18.5 – 24.9
Overweight25 – 29.9
Obese Class I30 – 34.9
Obese Class II35 – 39.9
Obese Class III40 and above

For children and teens under 18, BMI is interpreted using age- and sex-specific growth charts rather than adult cutoffs. This calculator therefore does not assign an adult category or healthy-weight range to people under 18. For personal health questions, consult a qualified healthcare professional.

How the calculations work

BMI = weight in kilograms ÷ height in metres squared. BMI Prime = BMI ÷ 25. Ponderal Index = weight in kilograms ÷ height in metres cubed.
