How to use this calculator

  1. Choose your date of birth.
  2. Select Calculate result to see your age in years, months and days.
Age is calculated by comparing your birth date with today's local calendar date, accounting for month lengths and leap years.

Frequently asked questions

Does the calculator use today's date?

Yes. It uses the current date on your device.

Can I enter a future date?

No. A future birth date is not accepted.

Is the result affected by timezone?

Near midnight, your device's local date may differ from another timezone.
